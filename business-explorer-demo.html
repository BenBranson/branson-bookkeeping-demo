Where these numbers come from

  • Starting cash , what customers owe by age, and vendor bills by age Books.
  • When that money moves is assumed, not taken from the books: Assumed.
  • New sales and costs at 's pace, spread evenly by week Calculated; new sales collected 4 weeks after the work Assumed. Labor costs are inside the weekly cost estimate.
  • Payroll dates and loan payments Missing. When real payroll dates are added, they replace the even labor estimate (never both). Loan principal is cash going out even though it isn't an expense, so it will be added separately.
